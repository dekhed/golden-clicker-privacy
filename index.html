Golden Clicker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Golden Games ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Golden Clicker mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Golden Clicker.

Information We Collect

Golden Clicker does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

The game may store certain gameplay and settings information locally on your device, such as your Coins, upgrade progress, game settings, and other gameplay preferences. This information is used only to provide and save your game progress and is not directly collected by us.

Advertising

Golden Clicker uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information from your device and interaction with advertisements, such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, approximate location information, and information related to ad interactions, in accordance with Google's policies.

This information may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, as well as to detect fraud and maintain the security of Google's advertising services.

Depending on your location, applicable laws, and your privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information about how Google handles information from apps that use its services, please visit: How Google handles information from apps and sites that use its services .

You can also learn more about Google's advertising practices at: Google Advertising .

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may process information as necessary to provide their services to the game. These services include:

  • Google AdMob — advertising and ad-related services.
  • Unity — the game development platform used to create Golden Clicker.

Third-party services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

Golden Clicker is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with Golden Clicker. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding the processing of your personal information, including rights provided by applicable privacy laws.

You may also manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your device settings and Google's available privacy controls.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Golden Clicker's privacy practices, you can contact us through the contact information provided on the Golden Clicker Google Play Store listing.
